Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries about studying MBBS abroad.

1. What are the eligibility criteria for MBBS abroad?

Eligibility varies by country but generally requires 10+2 with PCB subjects and NEET qualification.

2. What are the tuition fees for MBBS abroad?

Fees vary but are generally between $3,000 - $10,000 per year, depending on the country.

3. Is an MBBS degree from abroad valid in India?

Yes, if recognized by NMC/MCI, but you must pass the FMGE exam to practice in India.
